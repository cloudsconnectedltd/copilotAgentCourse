# HLE Welcome Buddy: conversation starters

Agent Builder calls these **starter prompts** (also shown as suggested prompts on the **Try it** tab). Each one has a short title and the prompt text that is sent when a user selects it. Agent Builder documents no minimum number of starter prompts and states no maximum (AB-03). This lab uses three.

| # | Title | Prompt text |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My vacation days | How many vacation days do I get in my first year, and how many can I carry over? |
| 2 | Get IT help | How do I contact the IT Help Desk, and what are its hours? |
| 3 | My first week | What should I do during my first week at Harbourline? |

Expected answers (from `data/answer-keys/hub-general.md`, section 1):

1. 15 vacation days in the first year (years 1 to 4). Up to 5 unused days can be carried over and must be used by March 31, or they are forfeited. Source: Vacation-Policy.docx.
2. Phone 1-800-555-0142, portal https://helpdesk.harbourline.example, email helpdesk@harbourline.example. Hours Monday to Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Eastern Time; 24/7 for outage-critical systems (press 1). Source: IT-Help-Desk-FAQ.docx.
3. Day 1 badge, laptop, meet onboarding buddy, set up MFA; Day 2 Safety Orientation course; Day 3 review benefits; Day 4 read the vacation guide and check the Workday balance; Day 5 New Employee Welcome Session. Source: Welcome-to-Harbourline.docx.
